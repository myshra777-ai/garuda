## 2. Pillar 1: Continuous Financial & Business Data Discrepancy Audit (The 48 Crore vs. 4.8 Crore Case Study)

### 2.1 How Cross-Team Number Discrepancies Happen in Code

Consider a real-world statutory reconciliation failure on **Total Claims Paid Amount (FY26)**:
1. **Claims Operations & Settlement Team (`ops.AggregateClaimsPaidCrore` in `internal/ops/claims_ledger.go:112`)**:
   - Reads raw Indian Rupees (`settled_claims_inr = ₹48,00,00,000`) from the settlement ledger.
   - Divides by `1e7` (`1 Crore = 10,000,000 INR`).
   - Correctly reports **`48.00 Crore INR`**.
2. **Finance & Executive Management Reporting Team (`finance.ExportExecutiveClaimsSummary` in `internal/finance/executive_report.go:76`)**:
   - Reads from an intermediate monthly rollup table (`ops_monthly_rollup.settled_claims_lakhs = 4,800 Lakhs`, where `1 Lakh = 100,000 INR`).
   - A developer or AI coding agent mistakenly divides Lakhs by `1000.0` (thinking of thousands) instead of `100.0` (`1 Crore = 100 Lakhs`).
   - Silently under-reports **`4.80 Crore INR`**—a **10.0x (₹43.20 Crore) statutory discrepancy**.

### 2.2 How Garuda Catches and Reconciles It Pre-Audit

Instead of waiting for external auditors to compare spreadsheets at quarter-end, Garuda inspects the AST and SQL data-lineage of every metric-producing function across repositories:

```text
│                    Formula: SUM(settled_claims_inr) / 1e7                   │
```

## 3. Pillar 2: Multi-Team Formula Disambiguation & 0.0–1.0 Weight Allocation

### 3.1 The Problem: Three Teams, Three "Loss Ratio" Formulas, Zero Documentation

In many companies, core financial and operational metrics evolve organically without a centralized rulebook. When Garuda scans an unorganized repository, it clusters functions that compute the same business concept across different team packages.

For example, for **Loss Ratio Calculation**, Garuda discovers three competing implementations:

| Team & Scope | Code Symbol & Location | Discovered Formula | Callers & Surface Criticality | What Breaks If Changed (Human-Readable Blast Radius) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ops Team** *(Claims & Underwriting)* | `ops.CalculateLossRatio`<br>`internal/ops/loss_ratio.go:42` | `(Incurred_Claims_Paid + LAE) / Earned_Premium` | **6 callers**<br>`LOW` Criticality<br>*(Harm Rank #1 — Least Harm)* | **Minimal Impact**: Only feeds the internal Ops Claims Queue and Underwriting Daily Digest UI box alignment. Zero customer or statutory outage risk. |
| **2. Marketing Team** *(Growth & Acquisition)* | `marketing.ComputeCampaignLossRatio`<br>`internal/marketing/campaign_roi.go:88` | `(Gross_Claims_Paid - Promo_Rebates) / (Gross_Written_Premium - CAC_Spend)` | **19 callers**<br>`MEDIUM` Criticality<br>*(Harm Rank #2 — Trade-Off)* | **Non-Blocking Feature Impact**: Connected to the Wishlist Promo Eligibility Loader and campaign banners. Core search and checkout remain unaffected. |
| **3. Management & Core Router** *(Pricing & Solvency Gate)* | `management.EvaluateEnterpriseLossRatioGate`<br>`internal/api/search_pricing_gate.go:194` | `(Total_Settled_Claims + IBNR_Reserves - Reinsurance) / Net_Earned_Premium` | **142 callers**<br>`HIGH_SEVERE` Criticality<br>*(Harm Rank #3 — Frozen)* | **Catastrophic Impact**: Wired directly into the synchronous Search Results Redirect & Checkout Dynamic Quote Gate. An incompatible change here takes down customer search redirection and quoting. |

### 3.2 How Garuda's 0.0–1.0 Weighting & Human-in-the-Loop Governance Works

Garuda enforces a strict **Human-in-the-Loop Mandate**: **AI is never allowed to silently guess or overwrite a business formula.**

Instead, Garuda computes a **0.00 to 1.00 relevance weight** based on which team context is actively working, combines it with the **Architecture Harm Rank**, and presents the options to the human decision-maker:

- **When working in the Ops Team Context (`--team ops`)**:
  - `ops.CalculateLossRatio`: **Weight `0.70` (70%)** — Recommended for local Ops standardization (Least Architectural Harm, `LOW` severity).
  - `marketing.ComputeCampaignLossRatio`: **Weight `0.10` (10%)** — `MEDIUM` severity trade-off.
  - `management.EvaluateEnterpriseLossRatioGate`: **Weight `0.10` (10%)** — `HIGH_SEVERE` (`FROZEN_SEVERE_DO_NOT_TOUCH`).
- **When working in the Finance / Management Context (`--team management`)**:
  - `management.EvaluateEnterpriseLossRatioGate`: **Weight `0.70` (70%)** — Highlighted as the authoritative statutory solvency formula, but locked behind human architect & CFO sign-off due to its 142-caller blast radius.
  - `ops.CalculateLossRatio`: **Weight `0.10` (10%)**.
  - `marketing.ComputeCampaignLossRatio`: **Weight `0.10` (10%)**.

## 4. Pillar 3: Auto-Synthesized Base Rulebook for Unorganized Companies

Most companies do not have clean, up-to-date Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) or formal financial formula catalogs when they first adopt Garuda. Writing those rules from scratch is tedious grunt work.

**Garuda `v1.9.9` does the grunt work for you—while keeping 100% of the authority with human stakeholders:**

1. **Automated Discovery**: Garuda scans your repository's AST call graph, fan-in hubs, SQL queries, and package boundaries to infer 6 foundational rules covering Surface Criticality Tiers (`SYNTH-CRIT-001..003`), Formula Weights (`SYNTH-LOGIC-004`), Crore Financial Normalization (`SYNTH-AUDIT-005`), and Hexagonal Store Isolation (`SYNTH-ARCH-006`).
2. **Human Review & Inline Editing**: Garuda writes the structured draft to `.garuda/DRAFT_BASE_RULEBOOK.md` and displays it in the **Auto-Synthesized Base Rulebook Studio** (`04 — Docs & Base Rulebook` in `/dashboard`). Finance leads and architects can edit any formula or threshold in plain Markdown.
3. **1-Click Approval & Cryptographic Seal**: Once reviewed, clicking **Approve & Seal Base Rulebook** (or running `./bin/garuda rulebook approve --by "CFO & Staff Architect"`) locks the document into `.garuda/APPROVED_BASE_RULEBOOK.md` with an RFC 6962 SHA-256 Merkle leaf hash and enforces it across all developer IDEs and CI pipelines.

## 5. Step-by-Step Workflows for Finance, Audit & RevOps Users

### Option A: Zero-Code Web Dashboard Workflow (Recommended for Finance & Audit Leads)

1. Open **`http://localhost:8080/dashboard`** in your browser and click **`09 — Financial & Logic Audit`** in the left sidebar (or click the red **`⚖️ Financial & Formula Audit (48Cr vs 4.8Cr)`** button in the top command center).
2. **Inspect Competing Team Formulas**:
   - Toggle between **Ops Team (0.70 Weight)**, **Marketing Team (0.70 Weight)**, and **Management Team (0.70 Weight)** at the top right.
   - Review the exact formula expression, caller count, and plain-English impact summary for each variant.
   - Click **`✓ Take Human Call: Approve`** on an approved formula or **`🔒 Keep Frozen`** on `HIGH_SEVERE` surfaces.
3. **Reconcile Financial Discrepancies (48 Cr vs 4.8 Cr)**:
   - Scroll to **Part 2: Cross-Team Business Number Discrepancy Detector**.
   - Review the side-by-side comparison (`48.00 Crore INR` vs `4.80 Crore INR` — `10.00x Mismatch`) and the **Garuda AST & Data-Lineage Audit Root Cause**.
   - Click **`✓ Approve Canonical Crore Fix`** to record your human audit reconciliation.
   - Click **`+ Simulate Custom Crore Audit Check`** to test custom team numbers interactively.
4. **Review & Approve the Base Rulebook**:
   - Click **`04 — Docs & Base Rulebook`** in the left sidebar.
   - Review or edit the synthesized rules in the **Editable Base Rulebook Markdown** studio, enter your name/title in the approver box, and click **`✓ Approve & Finalize Base Rulebook`**.

